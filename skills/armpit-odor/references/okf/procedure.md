# Armpit odor care orientation

SPDX-License-Identifier: CC-BY-4.0

<a id="nakabako.armpit-odor.routes"></a>
## Diagnosis is a checkpoint

腋臭症 (odor associated with apocrine secretions) and 原発性腋窩多汗症 (excessive axillary sweating) are distinct; they may coexist. An odor concern does not establish either diagnosis. JDA Q5 provides orientation; a clinician assesses symptoms and other causes.[^jda-odor] Do not infer diagnosis from earwax, ethnicity, family history or perceived smell. The JSPRS chapter rates diagnostic and surgery evidence as limited; gauze assessment can be used by clinicians but is not an agent self-test. Flap surgery has a weak recommendation/low-certainty evidence and operator-dependent results; microwave comparative evidence is limited and the chapter marks it uninsured. Reverify current reimbursement rather than assume approved means insured.[^jsprs-odor] Offer respectful assessment and a second opinion if uncertain; do not label the person's distress a psychological disorder.

## Keep four routes separate

| Route | Insurance boundary | Questions and tradeoffs for clinician discussion |
|---|---|---|
| Assessment and conservative care | Consultation/medicine coverage depends on diagnosis and service; OTC items are not automatically reimbursed | Discuss care, skin tolerance and expectations; do not prescribe deodorants, antiseptics or drugs |
| Clinician-indicated 腋臭症 surgery | K008 contains multiple categories, not only 剪除法; coverage is case/procedure/provider-specific | 皮弁法/剪除法 (flap approach), 皮膚有毛部切除術 and その他 are fee categories, not interchangeable technique recommendations |
| Treatment for diagnosed axillary hyperhidrosis | Some prescription and severe-condition botulinum toxin routes can be covered under their own indications; odor alone does not qualify | Verify current drug label, severity/previous-treatment requirements, contraindications, expected duration and repeats; no automatic patient eligibility |
| Cosmetic/self-pay procedures | Microwave/energy procedures and cosmetic injections must be checked individually; device approval is not reimbursement | Confirm exact device, target/site, approval/off-label status, alternatives, repeat costs and complication follow-up; no “scarless/risk-free/permanent” promises |

JDA's December 2023 amended guideline is for **sweating**, and describes prescription treatment paths; it is not an odor insurance protocol.[^jda-2023] The older Q11 discusses insurance for severe axillary hyperhidrosis botulinum toxin; recheck current labeling and billing before applying that historical statement.[^jda-btx]

MHLW's retrieved fee table lists K008: 皮弁法 6,870 points, 皮膚有毛部切除術 3,000 points and その他のもの 1,660 points. Use the current revision hub and implementation notices.[^mhlw-fee-hub] Use the current revision hub and implementation notices.[^mhlw-fee-hub] These are fee components, **not patient totals or evidence that any named cosmetic technique qualifies**. Verify the applicable current schedule/implementation notices, medical indication, exact technique, bilateral billing and provider before making an estimate. Do not multiply by two or assume a copay percentage.[^mhlw-k008]

The university hospital describes diagnosis before insured flap surgery and separates self-pay alternatives. Surgery can leave scars and cause hematoma, infection or tissue necrosis and requires follow-up/rest. Its old 2021 admission logistics and optimistic result wording must not be generalized or reused as current guarantees.[^nms]

The 2023 joint-society miraDry notice describes approved use for severe primary axillary hyperhidrosis and warns about inappropriate/off-site use and contraindications. Ask a clinician to explain current approval and consent; do not extrapolate use to genital/perineal areas or treat odor marketing as the approved indication.[^miradry-alert]

## Costs and recovery

Compare a defined horizon and outcome target: odor, sweat or both. Get separate written estimates for insured components and self-pay extras; check diagnosis, copay category, procedure, anesthesia, tests, medicines, dressings, return visits, admission, referral-related charges and optional items. Show transport, time off/arm-use limitations and any repeat treatments separately. Do not calculate insured totals from the surgery points alone or combine insurance/self-pay as if mixing were automatically permitted; have billing staff clarify the plan.

Discuss no intervention, conservative options, staged procedures or another consultation with the clinician. For surgery ask about scar/arm movement, wound/rest requirements, dressing/drain removal, suture visits, recurrence/residual symptoms and complication arrangements. For injections/energy procedures ask about pain/swelling, skin/nerve injury and repeat need; obtain current procedure-specific evidence, not invented incidence rates.

Use MHLW's informed-choice checklist and verify applicable contract cancellation rights, without promising cosmetic cancellation rules govern all insured surgery.[^mhlw-consent][^ncac-contract]
